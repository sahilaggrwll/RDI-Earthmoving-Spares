Questions buyers ask us

What earthmoving spare parts does RDI supply?

We supply replacement parts for excavators, loaders and other earthmoving and construction equipment: undercarriage parts, hydraulic parts, bucket pins and bushes, GET and bucket parts, hydraulic breaker parts, seal kits, filters, engine parts and other replacement parts.

Which machine brands do you supply parts for?

We supply parts suitable for JCB, Komatsu, Hitachi, Volvo, CAT, Hyundai, Kobelco, SANY and Doosan machines, and many more. RDI is an independent supplier. Brand names are used only to show machine compatibility.

How do I get a quotation?
